Fig. S1. Ingredient recommendation performance in herbal formulas and matched food samples. Separate plots show herbal performance and mean performance across 100 matched food samples. Bars represent Popularity, Mean conditional probability, and Mean pairwise Jaccard. Food error bars show the empirical 2.5th–97.5th percentiles across samples, not confidence intervals. Input conditions retain 2 or 3 ingredients, 50% or 75% of ingredients, or all but one ingredient (N−1). Performance is Recall@10, equivalent to Hit@10 for N−1. Scores are averaged within each composition and then equally across eligible compositions. Each cohort includes 1,899 eligible compositions for the 2-ingredient condition, 1,753 for the 3-ingredient condition, and 2,009 for each remaining condition.
